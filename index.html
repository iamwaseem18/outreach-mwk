Why it's different

Most outreach tools treat you like a bot. This one works like you do.

The cold-outreach market is full of cloud services that log into your account from their own servers and blast spun-text templates. Here's what changes that.

It runs where you do

Everything happens in a panel inside your own browser, signed in as you. Your LinkedIn session never gets shipped to someone else's server, so your account keeps behaving like a normal person using the site.

Every note is written, not spun

Connect Claude or Grok with your own API key. Each message is generated for one specific person from their role and company — not a template with a few synonyms swapped in.

Built for LinkedIn as it is today

It uses LinkedIn's own invitation flow, so it keeps working after interface updates that break older scripts the moment the DOM changes.

It knows when to stop

When LinkedIn requires someone's email to connect, the tool recognizes it and skips cleanly, instead of firing a broken request or hitting the wrong profile in the sidebar.

You stay in control

You operate it. Review and edit every message before anything sends, decide who makes the cut, and watch a live log of what happened — with human-paced gaps between each request.

Pay for usage, not a subscription

No monthly SaaS bill. You bring your own AI key and pay only for the tokens you actually use — usually cents per batch, not tens of dollars a month.

How it works

Three steps, start to sent.

1

Find people

Type a company name. The panel pulls everyone who matches straight from LinkedIn search, across pages, and removes duplicates as it goes.

2

Write the messages

Generate a personal note for each contact with your chosen model. Scan them, tweak any one inline, and keep the ones you like.

3

Send on your terms

Fire the connection requests with the note attached, spaced out like a human would. Watch a live log of sent, skipped, and failed.

Getting started

Up and running in a few minutes.

You install it once, add your own AI key, and you're the one driving from there.

1

Add it to Chrome

Install from the private Chrome Web Store link, or load the folder in Developer mode if you'd rather run it unpacked. It lives as a side panel next to LinkedIn.

2

Add your AI key

Open settings and paste an Anthropic (Claude) or xAI (Grok) API key. It's saved locally in your browser and used only to draft your notes — it never leaves your machine except to call that provider.

3

Find your people

Type a company name. The panel pulls everyone who matches from LinkedIn search and lists them for you, duplicates removed.

4

Draft the notes

Generate a personal message for each contact with your chosen model. Read them, edit any that need a tweak, and keep the ones you like.

5

Review, then send

Send the connection requests you approved, with the note attached and human-paced gaps between each. A live log shows what sent, what was skipped, and why.

Your account stays yours

No middleman ever touches your session.

Cloud automation services work by holding your LinkedIn credentials and acting from a datacenter IP that looks nothing like you — one of the fastest ways to get flagged. Outreach Assistant never asks for your password and never leaves your machine. The only outbound calls it makes are to the AI provider you chose, with the key you supplied.

Privacy

What the extension does and doesn't do with your data.

Plain-English version of the privacy policy. The short answer: it runs on your machine, and the only thing it sends anywhere is the text it needs to draft a note, to the AI provider you chose.

What it reads

Only the LinkedIn pages you open while using it — names, titles, and companies from the search results and profiles you're looking at. It reads nothing when you're not actively using it.

Where it's stored

Your contacts, drafted notes, settings, and API key stay in your browser's local storage on your own device. There is no account to create and no server that holds your data.

What it sends out

One kind of request only: the contact details and your prompt go to the AI provider you picked (Anthropic or xAI), authenticated with your own key, to draft each note. Nothing is sent to us.

What it never does

It never asks for or stores your LinkedIn password, never routes your session through a third party, never sells or shares your data, and includes no analytics or tracking.
